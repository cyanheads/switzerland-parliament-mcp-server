# 0.1.1 — 2026-10-25

## Added

- **`chparl_search_business` and `chparl_get_business`** discover business with committee and submission filters, then retrieve original texts and independently paged procedural records.
- **`chparl_get_votes`** discovers Nationalrat roll calls, pages member decisions, and computes complete vote and parliamentary-group counts from published member rows.
- **`chparl_search_transcripts`** follows debate subjects into original Bulletin passages, with scoped text searches and exact passage retrieval.
- **`chparl_search_members` and `chparl_get_member`** retrieve parliamentary affiliations, council terms, committee memberships, and declared interests using `PersonNumber`.
- **`chparl_list_reference`** resolves identifiers, coverage, languages, source dictionaries, and the official legacy/current status snapshot.
- **Source fidelity** preserves published text and HTML on both result surfaces, with source attribution, retrieval dates, whole-record pagination, and selectable large text fields.
- **Workflow deadlines** bound complete upstream workflows to 45 seconds, including joins, body reads, and transient retries.

## Dependencies

- **`entities`** added at `^8.1.0` for HTML-reference extraction.
- **`markdown-it`** added at `^15.0.3` as a development dependency for Markdown round-trip tests.
