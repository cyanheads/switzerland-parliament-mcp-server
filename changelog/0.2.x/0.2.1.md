# 0.2.1 — 2026-11-19

## Added

- **`chparl_get_business` `subjects` rows** carry `meetingDate`, `councilName`, `sessionId`, and `sessionName`. **`chparl_search_transcripts`** searches a business over 20 subjects when `council`, `sessionId`, or date bounds narrow it to 20 or fewer, and `council` accepts `Conseil des Etats` (unaccented, as the source publishes it). [#16](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/16)
- **`chparl_search_business`** takes `personNumber` and `role` to list a member's business through `BusinessRole`; one `role` reports `totalCount`. Committee searches no longer request a count they discard. [#17](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/17)
- **`chparl_get_votes`** member voting-history rows carry the roll call's `subject`, `billTitle`, `registrationNumber`, `meaningYes`, and `meaningNo`. [#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18)
- **`chparl_search_members` `asOf`** lists a committee's roster on a past day (`as_of_in_future` for a later date), and **`chparl_get_member`** section `committee_history` lists a member's past and current committee memberships. [#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/19)
- **`chparl_search_members` `interest`** finds members by declared interest, one row per declaration. [#20](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/20)
- **`chparl_list_reference` `query`** filters `committees`, `parties`, and `groups` by label or abbreviation in the selected language; `request_too_long` is declared there. [#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/21)
- **`chparl_search_business` `tagId`** filters by topic tag, and **`chparl_list_reference`** topic `tags` lists the tags. Without `committeeId` or `personNumber` a `tagId` search needs a submission window of at most 366 days (`tag_scope_required`), reports `totalCount` on its first page only, and sends one upstream request per continuation, so a continuation in an older one-year window finishes inside the deadline. [#22](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/22)
- **Interest declarations** carry `dateFrom`, on `chparl_get_member` section `interests` and `chparl_search_members` interest rows.

## Changed

- **Collection tools** return `limit` rows per page (capped at each tool's maximum) instead of cutting pages to a response byte budget; size notices remain only for single records. `chparl_get_votes` members mode defaults to 25 rows, down from 50. [#14](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/14)

## Fixed

- **`chparl_search_transcripts`** pages in debate order instead of passage-ID order. [#15](https://github.com/cyanheads/switzerland-parliament-mcp-server/issues/15)
- **Tool and field descriptions** no longer say `PersonNumber` differs from `Person.ID`; the source publishes them equal.
- **`chparl_list_reference`**'s empty-page notice advises revising session dates only for topic `sessions`, the one topic that takes dates.
