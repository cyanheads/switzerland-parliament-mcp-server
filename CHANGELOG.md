# Changelog

All notable changes to this project. Each entry links to its full per-version file in [changelog/](changelog/).

## [0.2.1](changelog/0.2.x/0.2.1.md) — 2026-11-19

Collection pages return the requested limit rows, debate passages page in debate order, and new filters find a member's business, past committee rosters, members by declared interest, and business by topic tag.

## [0.2.0](changelog/0.2.x/0.2.0.md) — 2026-11-15

A published switzerland-parliament-guide skill, committee rosters in chparl_search_members, debate-to-business links in chparl_search_transcripts, and per-section row schemas in chparl_get_business.

## [0.1.2](changelog/0.1.x/0.1.2.md) — 2026-10-25

Member voting history, canonical canton identifiers, and count-free author search.

## [0.1.1](changelog/0.1.x/0.1.1.md) — 2026-10-25

Seven read-only tools for Swiss parliamentary research.
